# PropLens Video Demo Script (2–3 Minutes)

This script is timed for a 2-3 minute screen-recorded walkthrough of PropLens. Read this naturally while showing the features on screen.

---

### **[0:00 - 0:30] Introduction & Rebranding**
* **Action on screen**: Show the home screen of the app. Move the cursor over the header logo **PropLens** and point out the refreshed trust badges ("Natural language search", "AI match reasons", etc.) and the updated stats row ("Instant AI Match" and "24/7 AI Advisor").*

> "Hi everyone! Welcome to my demo of **PropLens**, an intelligent AI-powered property search platform for Gurgaon, India. 
> 
> As you can see, the app has been rebranded to PropLens with a fresh, modern logo in the header. Right below the search bar, we’ve updated our core feature highlights to reflect our AI focus, such as 'Natural language search' and 'AI match reasons', and our hero statistics now show a live 'Instant AI Match' and '24/7 AI Advisor' indicator."

---

### **[0:30 - 1:15] Recent Searches & Natural Language Flow**
* **Action on screen**: Scroll horizontally through the "Recent searches" chips row. Tap on the chip `"3BHK near metro"`. Show the text box populate, the search run, and cards slide in with slide-up animations. Then type `"Villa in Sector 57 with gym"` in the search bar and click Search. Show the new chip get prepended to the recent searches list.*

> "Directly below the search input, we’ve built a 'Recent Searches' chip row. It loads defaults on first launch, is horizontally scrollable, and dismissible via the 'x' buttons. 
> 
> Tapping a chip like '3BHK near metro' instantly populates the search bar and triggers the matching algorithms. When we run a new custom search, like 'Villa in Sector 57 with gym', it automatically prepends the search term to our history chips, maintaining a rolling list of the last 5 queries."

---

### **[1:15 - 2:00] Filter Pills & Bottom Sheets**
* **Action on screen**: Click the "BHK Type" pill. Select `"3BHK"` and see the bottom sheet close and search run. Click "Budget" and select `"80L–1Cr"`. Note that the pills turn solid green with white text, and show the selected value (e.g., "3BHK", "80L-1Cr"). Click the clear 'x' on the Budget pill to show it reset and re-filter live.*

> "Below the search chips, we have our interactive Filter Pills row, starting with a fixed 'Filters' slider icon. 
> 
> Tapping any pill—for example, 'BHK Type'—opens a custom bottom sheet with selectable chips. When a filter is selected, the pill updates to show the selected value, like '3BHK', and turns solid green. The active filters are combined with whatever is typed in the search bar to refine matches. Clearing a filter is as simple as tapping the close icon on the pill itself."

---

### **[2:00 - 2:45] Property Details & AI Recommendations**
* **Action on screen**: Tap on the top property card (e.g. DLF Phase 1 or Sector 50 flat). The bottom sheet details page slides up. Point to the "Why this matches your search" AI section and show the live AI summary and investment, appreciation, and rental yield estimates.*

> "Now let's tap on our top-ranked property. This opens our property details view. Under the hood, the active filters are sent to our Gemma 3 model on OpenRouter as structured context. 
> 
> The LLM generates a custom, query-specific recommendation, detailing pros and cons, investment potential, and estimated rental yields tailored directly to our active query. It even extracts nearby schools and hospitals, presenting them cleanly in the UI."

---

### **[2:45 - 3:00] Conclusion**
* **Action on screen**: Scroll up to show the home screen one last time, highlight the voice input microphone icon, and wrap up.*

> "Finally, we have our voice input integration—by clicking the mic icon, users can speak their query naturally and let PropLens parse it instantly. 
> 
> Thank you for watching my demo of PropLens!"
